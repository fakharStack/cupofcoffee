Our Story

Crafting premium coffee and unforgettable moments in the heart of ELY.

The Beginning

Cup Of Coffee House was founded with a simple philosophy: to bring premium, artisan coffee and freshly baked goods to the heart of ELY. We believe that a great cup of coffee has the power to bring people together, inspire creativity, and make everyday moments special.

Our Quality Promise

We source our beans from the finest ethical farms and roast them locally. Our pastries are baked fresh every morning using locally sourced ingredients, ensuring that every bite is as memorable as every sip.

The Atmosphere

Step inside and feel the warmth. With a minimalist, modern aesthetic mixed with cozy touches, our cafe is the perfect spot for catching up with friends, working, or simply relaxing with a book.
